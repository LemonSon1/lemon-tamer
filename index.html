Timing settings

Five equal slots = one signal every 5.6 seconds.

Tap Enable Sound once on each device. Browsers require a user gesture before playing audio.

How the team uses it

  1. Everyone opens this same page and taps Enable Sound.
  2. Signal 1 through 5 are spaced evenly across the cycle. Each signal is a shared team cue, not permanently assigned to one person.
  3. A player can cover up to three cues with their available Muaves; teammates can cover the remaining cues. Agree on assignments in voice chat.

Default setting: a 28-second Muave cooldown, divided into five equal intervals (5.6 seconds). Change the cycle if your in-game timing requires a different interval.

Schedule is anchored to the device's UTC clock. Keep the page open and the device awake for reliable audio.
